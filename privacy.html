Privacy Policy

Pocket Ledger: Budget Tracker — Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Pocket Ledger: Budget Tracker. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Ledger handles information when you use the app.

Pocket Ledger is designed to help you track your personal finances, including income, expenses, budgets, categories, and profile information.

Information Stored in the App

Pocket Ledger may store information that you choose to enter into the app, including:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Occupation
  • Personal bio
  • Profile photo
  • Income records
  • Expense records
  • Budget information
  • Custom categories

This information is used to provide the features and functionality of the app.

Your Financial Data

Your financial records are stored locally within the app on your device.

Pocket Ledger does not send your personal financial records to an external server for storage.

Your transactions, budgets, categories, and profile information remain on your device unless you choose to use a future feature that specifically involves data transfer, backup, or synchronization.

Photos

If you choose to add a profile photo, Pocket Ledger requests access to your selected photo through the system photo picker.

The selected photo is stored within the app for displaying your profile picture. Pocket Ledger does not access your entire photo library.

Subscriptions and Payment Information

Pocket Ledger offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions, processed entirely through Apple's App Store billing system.

We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or other financial account information — this is handled directly by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices.

We do receive limited, non-financial information from Apple confirming subscription status (such as whether a subscription is active, in a free trial, or has expired), used only to unlock the relevant features of the app.

Data Sharing

Pocket Ledger does not sell, rent, or share your personal financial information with third parties.

We do not use your personal financial records for advertising purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information stored within the app. However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also protect your device with a secure passcode and use the security features provided by your device.

Future Features

Future versions of Pocket Ledger may include features such as cloud backup, data synchronization, data import and export, or additional subscription-based services. If these features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how information is handled by those services.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in an updated version of this policy, both on this page and within the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at tehreem_zaidi@icloud.com.
